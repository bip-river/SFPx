    Get a forest product permit

    Buy a permit online to collect firewood, a Christmas tree or mushrooms on Bureau of Wandering Lands public land.

    Demo. Nothing is charged, emailed or issued. Use fictional details.

    Start now

    It takes about 3 minutes.

    You’ll need

    • Your mailing address. The law requires it on every permit.
    • An email address.
    • A debit or credit card if your permit has a fee. You’ll pay on Pay.gov. Some permits are free.
    • A printer. Your permit isn’t valid until you print it and sign it.

    Good to know

    • Permits last from 7 days to 6 months, depending on the permit, or until the season ends if that’s sooner.
    • Firewood and Christmas tree permits usually include haul tags to attach to each load.
    • Permits are for personal use. All sales are final.
    • Some permits are sold only in person at a local BWL office.

        Need help?

        Pay.gov is down
        Wait 15 minutes and try again. You can also buy your permit at a local BWL office.
        I don’t see the permit I need
        We only show the permits and areas you can buy online right now. Some permits are sold in person or released by season. Contact your local BWL office for other options.
        My payment was declined
        Check your payment details and try again. If it keeps happening, wait a few minutes or contact your bank.
        I think I was charged twice
        Contact your local BWL office with your name, email address and permit reference.
        I can’t download or open my permit
        • Turn off pop-up blockers.
        • Try another browser or PDF viewer.
        • Check your device’s downloads folder.

        Still stuck? Contact your local BWL office.

        I don’t have a printer
        Download the package and print it at a library, a copy shop or a local BWL office. The permit must be printed and signed to be valid, and haul tags must be printed to cut out.
        I closed the page or lost my permit
        In production: a copy is also sent to your email. Permits can’t be looked up online, so contact your local BWL office during business hours. Staff can find your permit with your name and email address.
